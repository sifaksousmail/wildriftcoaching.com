Free replay drill: stop dying for vision

Imagine you die walking into an unwarded river before an objective. Pause the replay before you enter.

  1. Check who can follow. Your jungler is recalling, mid is clearing under tower, and two enemies are missing. No one can cover you.
  2. Pick a safer move. Wait on your side for a teammate. If you can't contest together, trade for a wave or tower elsewhere if it's safe.
  3. Track it for three games. Before every river entry without vision, ask who can follow. Review the decision even if you survive.

Which part should you start with?

Unsure what your role should do?

Start with your role chapter. Mark the first decision you get wrong repeatedly: a pathing detour, a missed roam window, a risky wave, or a fight you enter too early.

Start with macro and objective setup. In your next replay, pause at the wave or recall that kept you from arriving with your team.
