Wild Rift Farming Guide: Last Hits and Safe Gold

Free guide by Danny · Farming · 7 min read · Published September 6, 2026

Better farming in Wild Rift starts with two separate skills: finishing the minions in front of you and being in the right place to collect the next wave. You can last-hit well in lane and still fall behind by wandering through river while safe minions die at your tower.

This guide focuses on reliable income. The aim is to reach useful purchases while keeping enough health, time, and map access to use them.

Quick answer: find the farm you can collect and leave safely

Choose a safe wave, check whether a teammate already needs it, and decide where you will go after clearing it. During the wave, watch minion health instead of attacking continuously. Before taking another wave or camp, check whether it will delay a recall or an important team action.

1. Separate a missed last hit from a missed wave

A missed last hit happens while you are present: your attack arrives too early, you switch targets too late, or an ability leaves several minions low at once. A missed wave happens before you can attack anything: you recall late, follow a pointless chase, or walk to a lane another teammate has already cleared.

These need different fixes. Mechanical practice helps the first problem. Route planning and better decisions help the second. In your next replay, mark three lost farming opportunities with one of these labels before deciding what to practice.

A third category is an intentional sacrifice. Giving up a minion to avoid a dangerous trade can protect the rest of the wave. Do not count every minion you leave as an error.

2. Practice last hits with your champion's attack timing

Use Practice with your main champion. Watch how quickly allied minions lower an enemy minion's health, then time the finishing attack. For a ranged champion, account for the projectile's travel; for a melee champion, include the step into range. Repeatedly attacking the nearest minion hides these timing decisions.

Start with basic attacks. Once you can predict the next minion to die, add your normal farming ability. Notice whether it makes several minions die together and whether your next attack will be ready. Under your tower, observe how each turret hit changes the minion's health instead of relying on a universal attack sequence.

  • Practice for five minutes with the same champion and starting setup.
  • After each miss, name the cause: early hit, late hit, wrong target, or poor preparation.
  • Repeat with one adjustment, such as preparing a second minion before the first dies.

The target is fewer repeated misses in comparable conditions. An exact CS-per-minute target can hide differences between roles, matchups, and games.

3. Protect the next wave when trading

Suppose a low-health minion is within reach, but the enemy has moved forward with an engage ability available. Walking up may earn the last hit and cost enough health to force you out of lane. Judge the trade against the next wave too, especially when your escape tool is unavailable.

Try collecting with a suitable ranged ability if that leaves enough resources to stay safe. Otherwise, let the minion go. If this happens repeatedly, review your position before the minion became low: you may have stood on the wrong side of the wave or spent your defensive ability unnecessarily.

The laning phase guide covers the wider lane plan. For this drill, focus only on whether one attempted last hit made later farm inaccessible.

4. Assign the next wave before walking toward it

After a tower falls, three teammates often drift into mid while side waves go unanswered. Before leaving base, look at the incoming waves, your teammates' movement, and visible enemy threats. Choose a wave your champion can collect without duplicating someone else's route.

For example, if your mid laner is already moving toward a safe side wave, following them rarely creates a second farming opportunity. Another lane may need defending, or you may need to stay near the team. If every visible wave is assigned, use the time to reset or help your team move safely rather than racing an ally for the last hits.

An exposed side lane requires a separate safety check. Ask who can reach you, whether those enemies are visible, and which route lets you leave. A large wave beyond reliable vision is an invitation to inspect the map, not automatic permission to walk forward.

5. Route toward your next useful action

Plan a short sequence: collect this wave, recall, then approach the next team action. Do not keep extending it with one more camp and one more wave. The extra income becomes expensive if you arrive with unspent gold or force teammates to wait in a dangerous area.

If a fight starts during your route, estimate whether you can arrive while it is still playable. A nearby contest with allies ready to fight may justify leaving minions. A distant fight that has already collapsed usually does not justify abandoning safe farm to arrive alone. Use the recall timing guide when your farming route repeatedly delays purchases.

Farming can build a meaningful lead without kills. Riot's Patch 7.2 bounty update includes minion and jungle income in its assessment of gold advantage.

6. Adjust the income plan for jungle and support

As a jungler, connect available camps to where you want to finish your route. Crossing the map for an empty camp or hovering near a lane with no realistic play loses farming time. Before taking a lane wave, check whether its owner is returning and whether clearing it helps the lane state. Review repeated empty travel with the jungle pathing guide.

As a laner, check your jungler's route before taking a camp they are approaching. As a support, read your current support item's income rules and help your carry collect the wave safely. Avoid clearing through their last hits automatically. Clearing unattended minions to protect a tower is a different situation from competing with a carry who is present.

One replay drill: recover a single lost farming window

Find one minute where your income stalled. Trace where you walked, which waves were available, and what you gained by leaving them. Write one specific correction: “After recalling, check both side waves before walking mid.” Test it over your next three games, then check whether it recovered safe farm without making you late to important fights.

Farming FAQ

How do I improve farming in Wild Rift?

Separate missed last hits from missed farming windows. Practice finishing minions on your main champion, then review waves lost to late recalls, unnecessary grouping, and unsafe routes.

Should I farm or join a fight in Wild Rift?

Check whether you can arrive in time and contribute to a useful fight. Leave the wave for a realistic, important contest; collect safe farm when the fight is already lost or your team can disengage.

Should I take jungle camps as a laner?

Check your jungler’s route and the next map action first. Taking a camp they are approaching can waste their travel and delay their purchase. Prefer an unclaimed safe wave when it is available.

Should supports try to match a carry’s farm?

No. Supports should judge income around their role and current support item rules. Help the carry collect the wave safely, and clear unattended minions when needed to protect a structure or prepare a reset.
